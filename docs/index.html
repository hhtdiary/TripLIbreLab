TripLibreLab for iOS · by HHT Diary

Replay the days TripLibre recorded.

TripLibreLab turns a travel diary exported from TripLibre into a day you can play back on a map, then share as a picture once you have hidden what you'd rather keep private. TripLibre records your days; TripLibreLab is where you look back at them.

How it works

Import. Replay. Share.

The first release does one thing well: it takes a diary you already have and lets you watch a day of it again. Everything happens on your iPhone, without an account, a server, or TripLibre running at the same time.

01 · export

Export from TripLibre

In TripLibre, Settings › Create export writes a folder that includes a SQLite snapshot of your diary.

02 · import

Open it in TripLibreLab

Pick the file from the Files app. TripLibreLab checks that it can read it and, if not, tells you why.

03 · replay

Pick a day and press play

The day's trips and stops appear in order on a timeline and a map. Play, pause and scrub through it.

04 · share

Review, then share

Preview a summary image, hide or blur places and times you want to keep private, and share it with the iOS share sheet.

What you are looking at

Recorded, replayed, or made up? Always labelled.

A smooth animation can look more certain than the data behind it. TripLibreLab keeps four kinds of movement apart and says which one is on screen.

KindWhat it meansStatus
ObservedTrips, stops and GPS points as recorded and reconstructed by TripLibre.Release 1
ReplayedThe animation between recorded points. Where only a trip's start and end are known, the connecting line is drawn as schematic, not as a measured path.Release 1
SyntheticAn alternative day generated from explicit assumptions. Not observed, and not a prediction of what you will do.Planned
AdvisoryA comparison of alternatives against goals you set. An estimate with stated uncertainty, not a recommendation to change your life.Exploratory

Privacy

Nothing leaves the phone unless you share it.

TripLibreLab is designed to work offline, with no account and no server. It opens your export read-only and never uploads it.

A day of travel can reveal where someone lives, works and sleeps, even without a name on it. That is why sharing always goes through a preview where you can hide start and end places and exact times, and why nothing is shared automatically.

The export you import from TripLibre is a complete copy of your diary, raw GPS points included. Treat the file itself with care. The Data & privacy page explains what TripLibreLab reads, what it keeps, and what a shared image contains.
